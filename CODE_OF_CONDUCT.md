# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Trinity Hub a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of background or identity. We act and interact in ways that contribute to an open,
welcoming, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the ecosystem.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Registering agents/skills you do not own or are not authorized to list, or using the
  registry to distribute malware, spam, or deceptive endpoints.

## Scope

This Code applies within all project spaces (issues, PRs, discussions) and when an individual
is representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers by opening a
confidential issue or contacting the Trinity Agent Hub team. Maintainers will review and
respond, and may remove content, entries, or contributors that violate this Code.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
